How we help

Readiness first — placement by licensed professionals

We help you organize what you have and understand what may be needed. Coverage advice and policy placement are handled by licensed insurance professionals.

Who we help

  • General contractors
  • Subcontractors and specialty trades
  • Artisan trades — electrical, plumbing, HVAC, carpentry, finishing
  • Roofing, framing, masonry, and concrete operations
  • Contractors with fluctuating seasonal payroll
  • Contractors who rely heavily on subcontracted labor
  • Out-of-state contractors taking on work in New York, New Jersey, or Maryland

Common situations

  • Payroll has never been broken out by class code
  • The experience modification worksheet has not been reviewed against actual claim detail
  • General contractors are requesting certificates with specific language that cannot be produced quickly
  • Subcontractor certificates are missing, expired, or were never collected
  • An audit produced an unexpected result and the supporting records were incomplete
  • Work has expanded into new trades or new states without the file being updated
  • Job types and the percentage of work performed at height, or on new construction versus service, have never been documented
Get Ready

Documents we help you gather

✓Current workers' compensation declarations page
✓Payroll broken out by class code and by state
✓Experience modification worksheet
✓Loss runs, ideally three to five years
✓Subcontractor list with current certificates of insurance
✓Written subcontractor agreements, where they exist
✓Description of job types and approximate percentage of work by type
✓Federal employer identification number and entity formation documents
✓Any prior audit statements and supporting payroll records
